to my moon, from your sun

thank you for the mornings i get to wake up into, and for the nights you make feel less dark. thank you for being loud when i go quiet, and for going quiet with me when the world is too much.

i know the distance can make some days feel especially quiet, and lonely. i wish i could close every mile with a hug. until we can, remember that you're loved through every call, every message, and every day we spend getting closer to being together.

i counted the days in numbers because numbers are easy. but the real report is smaller and much bigger than that: the way you say my name, the way you fall asleep mid-sentence, the way a boring tuesday turns into something i want to remember.

we are not the same light. we were never supposed to be. you burn, I reflect — and between us there is an eclipse, and it is my favourite place in the world.

same sky. different light. same side, always.

yours, endlessly
